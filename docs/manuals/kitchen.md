# Kitchen manual

You use the kitchen display (KDS) and the kitchen printer.

## The screen

Each station sees its own tickets, oldest first, with table, time waiting and notes. Late
tickets change colour. Allergy and special notes are shown in bold on the ticket.

## Working a ticket

Tap an item to mark it **preparing**, then **ready** when it is at the pass. The waiter is
alerted when it is ready. When the waiter takes it, it is marked picked up.

## Out of stock

Press **Menu availability** at the top of the KDS. Find the dish (type part of its name, or pick
its category) and press its big **Out of stock** button. Every screen greys it out at once and
nobody can order it, not even from the table tablet or the QR menu. Portions of it on tickets you
have not started are cancelled at no charge (a CANCELLED slip shows them) and each waiter who
sent one gets an OUT OF STOCK alert for their table; what you are already cooking or have ready
stays. Press **Mark as available** when you have more. **Portions left** lets you give a count
instead: the dish sells out by itself at 0. Stock is for the whole dish, all its sizes.

## Cancelled and changed items

Cancelled items stay on the screen, struck through, so nothing is cooked by mistake. If the
printer or screen is not working, tell the manager; tickets are not lost and print when the
printer is back.

## You cannot

Change prices, take payment or void an item. Ask a manager.
