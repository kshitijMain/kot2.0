# Waiter's manual

You use the waiter app on your phone, and sometimes a wrist pager.

## Sign in

Open the app and enter your PIN. The phone must stay on the restaurant Wi-Fi. Keep
notifications on and the phone charged; the manager has shown you the battery settings.

## Your tables

There are no sections: you may serve any table. Home shows every table by floor area; press
**Tables I sent KOTs to today** to see only the tables you sent orders to. Everything you do is
recorded under your name.

## Taking an order

Tap the table, add items with their variants and modifiers, add notes, and send. If the phone
loses Wi-Fi your order waits on the phone and is sent when it reconnects; it is never sent
twice. The prices shown are the system's; you cannot change them.

## Customers ordering themselves

A guest may order from the QR code or the table tablet. Orders that need your approval appear
as an alert to every waiter on duty; approve or decline. Any waiter may approve any table's order.

## Alerts and pager

You are alerted when a dish is ready, a guest asks for you (water, bill, help) or a manager
calls. Acknowledge so the kitchen knows you saw it. A guest request you do not answer is passed
to a manager. Your wrist pager buzzes for the same things.

## Serving

Mark an item picked up when you take it from the pass and served when it reaches the table.

## Changes

You may cancel an item before the kitchen starts it. After cooking has started you need a
manager's PIN. You may move or merge any table. You cannot give discounts or take payment: ask a
manager or request the bill from the table screen.

## Lost phone

Tell the manager at once so the phone is revoked.

## Out of stock

A dish the kitchen marked out of stock is greyed out with "Out of stock" and cannot be added. If
it was on a KOT you sent and the kitchen had not started it, it is cancelled at no charge and you
get an **OUT OF STOCK** alert for that table (dish and quantity): tell the guests and offer
something else. If the kitchen had already started a combo with it, the alert says so: talk to
the guests and a manager.
